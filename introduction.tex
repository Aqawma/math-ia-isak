\section{Introduction}

I have spent my entire life living on a hay farm, where we make small square bales. These bales are approximately 22 kilograms in mass and 91, 41, 36 centimeters in length, width, and height. These bales are tied with two lengths of twine by a machine called a baler. A photo of a bale is given in Figure \ref{im:haybale}.
