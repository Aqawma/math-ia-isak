\section{Background}
\subsection{Making \& Binding of Square Bales}
To understand the mathematics that appears later in the paper, one must first understand three things. First, the baler compresses the loose hay along the bale's length when it is being made. Second, the twines run lengthwise. This means that the twine restrains the bale's spring back in the same direction as the hay was originally compressed. Finally, the loops of twine sit at different positions across the width of the bale.
